My day-to-day responsibilities are to work on the tasks assigned to me.

For these past 4 months I was primarily working on two major projects: 

### Insignia Website Redesign

I was tasked with implementing the design of the new website for the company. I implemented the design entirely myself with Blazor Server

This project was a fun challenge. I had previously created a website from scratch but had no real
direction. Having a design provided to me by the company's marketing department made it much easier as that entire UI/UX of the site was offloaded to somebody else. 

Another aspect that was new to me was the development -> demo -> improvement cycle. Every so often I would show my peers and boss the site as it was in development and there would be changes, large or small, that they wanted me to implement. Having to work with actual business requirements was a good change of pace and made me realize the benefit of collecting your client's requirements before beginning a project. This experience is very similar to what we're learning in CIS*3750 right now. 

Overall, I enjoyed creating the site and I am very happy I was able to contribute something of lasting consequence in my time at the company. 

### NCIP

NCIP is a library to library communication protocol that reduces manual work and allows for library items to be shared across consortiums with the ease of automation. 

For this project I was tasked with creating an implementation of the NCIP protocol that was 
fully compatible with one of our partner companies. 

This project had been in essentially development hell for quite some time. It was originally started by someone no longer at the company. Then it was passed down to my current boss who completed a large portion of it but for another subset of the NCIP protocol (i.e. not what was needed now). 

I learned of this project in the summer but had not spent my time working on it. I spent most of my time then learning about how the protocol works. 

During this work term, I given more specific requirements and felt I was in a place where I had enough knowledge of the project to work on the implementation. 

I worked on the implementation and got it to a state where we are ready to integrate with the partner company. At the end of the work term I was waiting on our partner and therefore could not complete the project.
